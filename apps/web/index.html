How a match works

  1. Hold to fire, drag to aim

    The cannon fires one dude every 0.4 seconds while your thumb is down. Let go and it stops.

  2. Every gate starts as a ?

    Most multiply (×2 to ×5, or +N). Some hurt (÷2, ÷3, or −N). A gate flips face-up the first time your dudes touch it. Once per stage you can scout one in private.

    ?×2 ?÷2 ?+10

  3. Charge a Champion

    Every shot charges the meter, and it fills after 8 seconds of firing. Let go with it full and a giant worth 8 dudes marches out. A ×3 gate makes it three giants.

  4. Towers, then the town hall

    Fight 1 has one tower. Fight 2 has two, guarding the town hall. Drop the last one and you breach: for 3.5 seconds, every dude that reaches the hall scores.

  5. Don't get overrun

    The base sends troops back down the lane. If a single one reaches your cannon line, you lose the whole stake.

Same level, real opponent

Both of you get the same level, dealt fresh each match, and play it at your own pace. A thin bar shows how far they've got. Every match ends by 3:00.

Why neither side can cheat

The match runs inside a MagicBlock Private Ephemeral Rollup and settles on Solana. There's no game server holding the answers.

Sealed gates
Unrevealed gate values sit in private accounts. Your opponent's client can't read them, and neither can yours.
Dealt by VRF
Verifiable randomness picks the level and the gates, so neither player can stack the deck.
A clock on-chain
The match ticks 20 times a second on the rollup with no gas, and commits once at the end.
Pays out by itself
The result settles on Solana without anyone pressing a button. Afterwards the seed is public, so anyone can rebuild the level and check every gate.
The catch
Privacy relies on the rollup's validator running inside Intel TEE hardware, so you're trusting that hardware. It's devnet SOL only for now.
