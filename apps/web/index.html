How a match works

Two players. The same level. Whoever wrecks the base harder takes the pot.

  1. HOLD

    Hold to fire, drag to aim

    Your cannon pours lil dudes down the lane while your thumb is down.

  2. ?

    Hidden gates

    Most multiply (×2 to ×5, +N), some hurt. A gate flips face-up only when your dudes touch it.

  3. ×8

VS

Same map. Real opponent.

You both fire at identical copies of the same level, dealt fresh every match. A race bar shows how far they've got. The clock caps every match at 3:00.

  • Score tower damage, breach first, rush the hall
  • Win by a lot, take a lot: payout scales with the margin
  • Draws give both stakes back

Fair by construction

No server you have to trust with the secrets. The match runs inside a MagicBlock Private Ephemeral Rollup and settles on Solana.

1

Sealed gates

Unrevealed gate values live in private accounts. Nobody can read them until the match ends, not even your opponent's client.

2

Dealt by VRF

Verifiable randomness picks the level and the gates, so neither player can stack the deck.

3

On-chain clock

The match ticks 20 times a second on the rollup, free of gas, with one commit at the end.

4

Pays out by itself

The result settles on Solana automatically. Hit Verify to replay the match from its seed.
